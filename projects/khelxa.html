UI/UX Design

Khelxa

A sports media platform concept solving Nepal's live sports streaming challenges — buffering, live delay, and unreliable platforms — through a premium, user-first design.

Figma UI/UX Design User Research Wireframing Prototyping Design Systems
The Problem

Nepali sports fans lack a dedicated, high-quality platform for national and international sports coverage. Existing solutions have poor UX, unreliable streams, excessive buffering, long live delays, and no localisation for Nepal's sports ecosystem — leaving fans frustrated.

The Solution

A fully-designed Figma concept proposing a clean, intuitive interface built around a reliable streaming architecture. The design is research-backed — informed by user interviews and competitive analysis — and includes a full design system with dark-first colour palette, bold typography, and micro-animations.

Key Screens
—Home feed with live, upcoming, and recent matches
—Live watch screen with real-time scores and commentary
—Sports category browsing: Football, Cricket, Volleyball
—Player and team profile pages
—News and highlights section
—User profile and subscription management
Challenges
→Designing for a streaming context where latency and buffering feedback is critical to UX
→Creating a design system that scales across mobile, tablet, and smart TV viewports
→Balancing content density with readability on live match screens with real-time data
Lessons
↑User research revealed mobile is the primary device — the design was restructured mobile-first
↑Dark-first design reduces eye strain and improves focus during long live-match sessions
↑Micro-animations in prototypes significantly improve the quality of stakeholder feedback
